iPhone iOS 17/18. iOS needs AdGuard app (free) for system-wide DNS filtering.
1

Install AdGuard — free on App Store

Search "AdGuard" → download the free version.

2

Enable DNS protection

AdGuard → DNS Protection → toggle ON. Allow VPN config — stays on your device only.

3

Add our blocklist

DNS Protection → DNS Blocklists → Add custom list → paste URL:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/x0uid/SpotifyAdBlock/master/SpotifyBlocklist.txt
4

Force-close & reopen Spotify
